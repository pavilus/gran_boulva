One-page investor business plan

Haitian opinions turned into influence, status, and eventually income.

Gran Boulva is a mobile-first social debate and prediction platform for Haitian and diaspora communities. It does not compete by asking users to produce polished video. It competes by organizing the behavior already happening every day: arguing, voting, predicting, joking, ranking, defending culture, and following the people whose opinions move the room.

Core Problem

Haitian digital conversation is intense but fragmented across TikTok comments, WhatsApp, Facebook, YouTube, and X. The audience has voice, emotion, and frequency, but no dedicated system that structures debate, surfaces credible community influence, or helps creators monetize opinion.

Real Behavior

The product taps into identity, public reputation, rivalry, prediction, humor, politics, music, sports, and diaspora belonging. The psychological engine is simple: people return when their voice earns visible status inside a culture they care about.

Category

A culturally specific social influence network: part Reddit, part prediction game, part creator economy, part community media platform. Strategically, it resembles a niche social graph with game mechanics and monetizable creator identity.

Product Strategy

  • MVP: matchup voting, debate arguments, topic feed, profiles, rankings, streaks, share cards, reporting, basic admin moderation.
  • Do later: paid creator subscriptions, complex AI recommendations, advanced coins economy, live debate, heavy marketplace features.
  • Retention: daily matchups, leaderboards, prediction settlement, reply notifications, identity-based badges, creator ranking resets.
  • Moat: Haitian cultural topic graph, reputation history, creator influence rankings, moderation norms, and topic prediction data.

Market & Users

  • Primary: Haitian Gen Z and millennial diaspora users in the U.S., Canada, France, Haiti, Dominican Republic, and Caribbean hubs.
  • Power users: people who already debate politics, music, soccer, relationships, diaspora identity, and community drama online.
  • Creators: micro-influencers, commentators, meme pages, music voices, radio personalities, podcasters, student leaders.
  • Reachable near-term market: tens to low hundreds of thousands before expansion; the 15M+ population headline is aspirational, not immediate TAM.

Growth

Start where Haitian attention already lives: TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp groups, Facebook communities, YouTube comments, campus associations, churches, music events, and diaspora media pages. Best loops: result cards, creator rankings, prediction receipts, weekly Top 10, creator-hosted drops.

Business Model

Strongest early monetization is status-driven digital goods: boosts, profile cosmetics, creator support, prediction badges, and sponsored debates. Subscriptions and ads should come later. Bad monetization turns visibility into pay-to-win before trust exists.

Investor Test

Fundable when it proves daily habit and organic sharing: 25K+ registered users, 3K-5K DAU, 20%+ week-4 retention, 30%+ posts/comments from returning users, controlled toxicity, and early coin conversion or sponsor revenue.
