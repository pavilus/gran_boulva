## FULL DESCRIPTION — ENGLISH (max 4000 chars)

Gran Boulva is Haiti's first social debate and prediction platform — where real conversations about Haitian culture, politics, sports, and current events happen every day.

**VOTE ON MATCHUPS**
Every day, hot topics from Haitian news are turned into A vs B matchups. Pick your side and see how Haiti thinks. Your vote unlocks the debate — then read what others said and add your voice.

**POST YOUR ARGUMENTS**
After voting, share your argument with the community. The best arguments rise to the top — boosted by coins from supporters who agree with you. Be the voice that moves the crowd.

**MAKE PREDICTIONS**
Think you know what's going to happen? Make predictions on trending topics — from sports outcomes to political events — and build your reputation as Haiti's top forecaster.

**EARN COINS & BADGES**
Engage every day to earn coins, build streaks, and unlock badges. There are 10 badge tiers — from first vote to Cultural Icon. Coins let you boost arguments, support other voices, and unlock premium features.

**BECOME A CREATOR**
Active community members earn Creator status with revenue sharing, exclusive debate access, and a verified badge. Climb from Rising Creator to Verified Creator to Elite — and beyond.

**POWERED BY AI**
Gran Boulva's AI Scout scans Haitian news sources daily to generate fresh, relevant debate topics — so you're always talking about what matters right now in Haiti.

**DESIGNED FOR HAITI**
Built by and for the Haitian community. Haitian Creole (Kreyòl) is the primary language. Covers culture, politics, sports, entertainment, society, education, environment, technology, and more.

---

Key features:
• Daily A vs B matchups drawn from real Haitian news
• Voter-gated debates — vote first, then read the arguments
• Coin economy: earn through daily streaks, referrals, and rewards; spend on boosts and support
• 10-level badge system with XP
• Creator tier system with revenue sharing (60–80%+)
• Follow system and Top Voices leaderboard
• Predictions and forecasting on trending topics
• Boost system to amplify the best arguments
• Haitian Creole first; English fully supported
• Dark mode only — sleek purple and black design

---

**COMING SOON — DEBATE BATTLES**
Gran Boulva Debate Battles: live 1-vs-1 video debates streamed to the whole community. Challenge another user, debate in front of a live audience, and let Haiti decide the winner. Coins on the line.

---

For Haitians at home and in the diaspora who want to stay connected, share their opinions, and influence the national conversation.
