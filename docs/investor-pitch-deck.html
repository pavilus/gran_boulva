01 Problem

Haitian conversation is massive, emotional, and fragmented.

Scattered

Debates live across TikTok comments, WhatsApp groups, Facebook, YouTube, and X.

Unstructured

Good opinions disappear. There is no trusted scoreboard for community voices.

Unmonetized

Creators generate engagement, but general platforms capture most of the value.

The audience has attention and voice. It lacks dedicated infrastructure.
Gran Boulva
02 Solution

A structured public square built around opinions, predictions, and influence.

Vote

Two-sided matchups turn culture into fast participation.

Argue

Top arguments create reputation, not just noise.

Predict

Users build credibility by being early and right.

Earn Status

Rankings, badges, streaks, and creator profiles make influence visible.

Opinions become social currency.

04 Product

MVP focused on habit, not bloat.

Build First

  • Matchups and voting
  • Argument threads
  • Profiles and rankings
  • Prediction settlement
  • Share cards
  • Moderation/admin tools

Build Later

  • Creator subscriptions
  • Advanced recommendation AI
  • Live debate rooms
  • Complex paid visibility markets
  • Regional expansion categories

Product principle: make reputation compound before monetization becomes heavy.

Gran Boulva
05 Business Model

Status-led monetization.

Boulva Coins

Boosts, creator support, cosmetics, streak recovery, and premium participation.

Creators

Support fees, subscriptions, premium communities, live debate events, sponsorship tools.

Brands

Sponsored matchups, prediction campaigns, event partnerships, native cultural advertising.

The early monetization rule: sell identity and support, not unfair dominance.
Gran Boulva
06 Growth Strategy

Growth starts where Haitian attention already lives.

Creator Seeding

Recruit 20-50 founding voices across music, politics, comedy, sports, and diaspora life.

Share Loops

Matchup results, rankings, prediction receipts, and debate callouts built for TikTok, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

Community Partnerships

Diaspora events, campus groups, podcasts, DJs, radio hosts, meme pages, and cultural organizations.

Avoid broad paid acquisition early. Use culturally specific topics that people feel compelled to answer publicly.

Gran Boulva
07 Technology

AI supports the community; it does not replace it.

Recommendation

Personalized feed balancing interest, discovery, and perspective broadening.

Boulva Scout

Trend-sourcing engine for Haitian news, social content, events, and debate prompts.

Moderation

AI-assisted toxicity detection, reports, trust scores, and human review for sensitive topics.

Long-term data advantage: topic graph, reputation graph, prediction history, and creator influence graph.

11 Vision

From Haitian debate app to cultural influence infrastructure.

If Gran Boulva wins, it becomes the place where Haitian voices are ranked, discovered, supported, and monetized. The expansion path is Caribbean and diaspora communities that share the same need: culturally fluent social infrastructure where identity, opinion, and influence matter.

The long-term asset is not the feed. It is the trusted map of who moves culture.
