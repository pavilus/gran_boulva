# Gran Boulva Canva Matchup Template

Use this handoff when the generated option images are good but the final poster
needs a human-designed Canva composition.

## Canva Files

Create two Canva designs:

| Asset | Canva size | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poster | `1080 x 1350 px` | Admin gallery and downloadable marketing poster |
| Share | `1200 x 630 px` | Social link preview image |

Import these guides into Canva:

- `docs/canva_templates/matchup_poster_template.svg`
- `docs/canva_templates/matchup_share_template.svg`

Use `assets/images/composite.png` as the visual reference for the poster style.
The SVG guides provide layer positions and editable placeholder copy.

## Required Assets

Download from the matchup admin image section:

1. `Opsyon A`
2. `Opsyon B`

Upload into Canva:

1. The two option images
2. `assets/images/logo_email.png`
3. `assets/images/fire.png`

## Poster Layer Order

1. Background color: near-black violet.
2. Option A image clipped to the left panel.
3. Option B image clipped to the right panel.
4. Dark tint over both panels so white text stays readable.
5. Slanted magenta center split.
6. Top `Matchup Popile` pill.
7. Matchup question.
8. CTA: `Vin di saw panse sou Gran Boulva`.
9. Tagline: `Debat. Vote. Agimante.`
10. Left and right answer cards with the `VS` mark between them.
11. Footer brand block and App Store block.

## Copy Rules

- Keep the matchup question to three lines when possible.
- Button copy is only `Wi` and `Non`.
- Do not write `Vote Wi` or `Vote Non`.
- Brand copy is `Gran Boulva` and `Vwa ou konte`.
- The CTA must be `Vin di saw panse sou Gran Boulva`.
- The tagline must be `Debat. Vote. Agimante.`

## Export

Export both files from Canva as PNG:

1. Poster PNG at `1080 x 1350 px`.
2. Share PNG at `1200 x 630 px`.

On the admin matchup page, upload both finished PNGs in the `Imaj Matchup`
section. The poster is used for the admin gallery and public poster preview.
The share image is used by the matchup share page metadata.
